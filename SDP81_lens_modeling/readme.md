This image displays part of our lens modeling results of SDP81 observed by the ALMA long baseline campaign (<a href="https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2041-8205/808/1/L4/meta" target="_blank">ALMA Partnership et al. 2015b</a>). We used an Elliptical Power Law (EPL) density profile, external shear, and lens multipole density as the lens model, and shapelets (<a href="https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/338/1/35/1096986" target="_blank">Refregier 2003</a>) with regularization (<a href="https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/457/3/3066/2588894" target="_blank">Tagore & Jackson 2016</a>) as the source model. We used the ALMA image-plane likelihood function (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08393v2" target="_blank">Zhang et al. 2025</a>) for lens modeling. The residual images show an overall good fit.
